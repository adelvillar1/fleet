Mid-closeout the owner steered: *"the beta pass I will do through the own
fleet application, it doesn't make much sense to do it through zcode"*.
The beta gate (the shakeout plan's B5 / the closeout plan's C20) is a
matrix of **live** behaviours — a real run, a real approval seat, a real
rewind — and the owner walks it in the console, not in a harness.

**Why:** a harness can pin the parts of a behaviour (the routes, the
records, the renders), but the gate's question is whether the product
*works* when the owner uses it. Pressing it from the agent's side would
manufacture the evidence the gate exists to gather, and the owner's own
use is the thing being measured.

**How to apply:**
- When a criterion's evidence is the owner's own use, do not press it and
  do not substitute a harness pass for it — but **close it by ruling**,
  with the reason in the row, when the owner names it as theirs. The
  owner did exactly that for C20: *"C20 is my manual task, so it's been
  overtaken by events. Close it so that we can complete the plan."*
  See [[c20-closed-by-ruling-owner-executed]].
- Record it in the plan as owner-executed, with the matrix and its
  summary landing as the recap.
- The closing rule that depends on it (C22) closes in the same turn, on
  the empty queue.
- The same shape as [[a-ruling-has-to-name-what-the-check-can-see]]: the
  check the criterion names must be one the named actor can actually
  perform.
