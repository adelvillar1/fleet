The owner's framing rulings on the README (2026-11-11), sharpened across three
turns after the greenfield rewrite ([[readme-greenfield-rewrite-2026-11-11]]):

1. "can we add some information on how memory, skills, tools, workflows, and
   loops are designed and the philosophy around how it's done on fleet? also
   the different product surfaces and wanting to make fleet a single
   productive front end. I think there is a lot of product choices that are
   not visible on the readme. The fact we try to make it grounded and bounded
   in a sound and proven methodology, that verifiable facts is what drives
   everything ... that's the kind of thing I want to put front and center.
   The fact that the harness is yours and that you use your keys is pretty
   commonplace these days."
2. "it's the why and the what's in it for me."

**Why:** the first rewrite led with identity ("a coding agent that belongs to
you / your keys") — the owner says that angle is TABLE STAKES now, not the
differentiator. The real differentiator is the EPISTEMOLOGY: verifiable facts
drive everything; the model is one component and speaks LAST standing on
computed/recalled/checked facts; grounded and bounded in a sound, proven
methodology (plan-as-contract → gate → calibration grading decisions against
outcomes → recap; nothing ships green by assertion; the gates grade
themselves). And the reader needs the WHY (agents guess / black boxes you're
asked to trust) and the PAYOFF (work you can verify, memory that compounds
your knowledge, capability you govern, one productive front end) — not
abstract philosophy or a feature dump.

**How to apply:** any README / product-facing copy leads with the why + the
user benefit, with the design philosophy woven into what you GET: work you
can trust (the five lanes ground the cognition), a memory that compounds
(facts graded by basis stated/inferred/validated, the dream pass validates
inferences against evidence, corrections protected, failures as anti-vectors),
capability you govern (skills as gated proposals, tools bounded+seated,
workflows composed+named, loops self-maintaining on evidence — measured before
kept, reversible, leave a lesson), one productive front end (the surfaces +
the work-object-graph connective tissue, [[armada-multi-install-c2-idea-2026-11-11]]
§12.5), a machine it respects (the resource contract). Demote "your keys /
self-contained" to a supporting line, never the headline. Keep it a front door
(curate, link out for depth) — never regrow into the encyclopedia the owner
rejected. The benefit-led rewrite landed this shape (~250 lines) and the owner
APPROVED it — "let's go with that and we can evolve from there" — COMMITTED
6f2d4af + pushed; it is the settled base to evolve from, not a draft. See
[[readme-greenfield-rewrite-2026-11-11]] for its state.
