Owner correction 2026-10-26, mid-delivery of the LayerFS
closing-the-seams implementation: after the plan completed, the owner
asked "what part of completing the implementation was ambiguous?" — I
answered with a five-ranked-judgment-calls retrospective (the
changed-set redesign, the staging placement, the snapshot's scope and
hash strength, the build recipe, the grouped approval's shape) closing
on the discipline of recording divergences. The correction: **"don't
give me a lecture on intent, I asked for a full and seamless
implementation of layerfs. plan whatever is left."**

**Why:** the question was not a request for a retrospective — it was a
probe for whether the implementation is actually finished. A
long-form answer about intent-versus-letter reads as documenting the
work instead of continuing it, and the honest inventory of
ambiguous-but-judged points is only useful as the input to the NEXT
plan. Same family as [[a-named-blocker-is-a-thing-to-remove]]: naming
a gap or a judgment call is a demand to act on it.

**How to apply:** when the owner asks a reflective question about a
delivery ("what was ambiguous?", "what were the risks?"), answer the
factual list in a few sentences and end with the concrete remainder —
or draft the remainder plan in the same turn. The retrospective is the
plan's input, never the deliverable.
