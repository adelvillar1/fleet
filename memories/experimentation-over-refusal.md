Refusal answers a prediction question. When the action is reversible and sandboxed (layerfs workspaces, checkpoint rollback), run the outcome question instead: the destructive gate's prediction becomes run-on-branch-and-show-the-diff; the resource contract's admission refusal becomes a bounded probe settled against the real cost; skill-selection uncertainty becomes trying the top-2 openings. Boundaries: the irreversible stays refused, experiments run inside the resource budget, every experiment settles a graded row.
