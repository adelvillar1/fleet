The owner's framing, verbatim:

> *"a capability gap I view it as an opportunity to create a custom persona
> with the skills that are needed, nothing constrains the huddles to the few
> roles we have. Imagine in real life you have a company and you need an
> expert in quantum physics, do you abandon your project or hire a consultant"*

**Landed the same turn (e15a4a7):** the gap record widened to
`(name, kind, reason, from)` where kind is `role` / `tool` / `skill` /
`capability`, because the kind decides what the owner can do about it. A role
the home keeps convenes at once; a tool, a skill, or an unnamed capability
offers to **hire for it** — an authoring door prefilled from the gap writes
the persona through the same role writer the settings page rides, joins it to
the room, and leaves it in the home's roles for every later huddle. The
consultant is on the payroll after the meeting.

**Why:** a gap is not a dead end, it is the room telling you it needs someone
it does not have — and Fleet's role machinery (`roles::write`, `RoleFields`,
the settings role op) already makes authoring one a form, not a code change.
Treating it as a blocker made me offer fixes for the *symptom* (a convene
press) when the owner wanted the *capability* (a new hire).

**How to apply:** when a room hits something it lacks, the question is never
"which existing role covers this" — it is "who do we need, and what does that
person carry". Nothing constrains a huddle to the six roles, and a persona the
room authors is a first-class member, not a workaround. This supersedes the
narrower reading in [[a-delivery-is-not-a-file]], which still stands for the
*other* kinds (a decision requested, a review, a discussion request).

**The mechanism was superseded the same day.** The owner-pressed card built
