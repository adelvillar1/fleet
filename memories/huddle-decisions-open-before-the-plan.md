This file survives as the record of what was open and how it was settled.
What was settled by the owner, and what was still open:

**Settled by the owner:** the Slack shape; members see every message; `@` is
a directive/ask/review-request and bots may request reviews from each other;
bots bring their **skills** (assigned + ranked for the problem shape), not
their own tool sets; one session per huddle; breakout huddles for
multi-phase work; the destructive directive stays with **sys1** as the
judge; the composer keeps **ask / autonomous / full access**; a bot's name
is free and its avatar optional; a bot's soul is optional; and the owner's
**SOUL is the general guardrail over the bots** — each bot keeps its own
voice within it, bounded by truthful, honest, respectful, solution-oriented
([[a-bot-soul-is-its-role-overlay]]).

**Still open (the decisions that change the build):**

1. **Does a mention auto-run the bot, or ask the owner to pass the turn?**
   The middle option was offered: bot→bot mentions auto-run inside a turn
   budget, `@you` always waits. This is the first decision to settle — it
   decides whether the owner is the router or a participant.
2. **Does a bot bring its own tools, or share the huddle's?** The owner's
   skills ruling points at shared tools, but the role's `tools` field and
   the delegation path both carry per-role tool narrowing, so the two are
   not yet reconciled.
3. **Where the section sits** — a fourth `work` destination beside sessions
   (the owner's first instinct), or the existing `bots` destination moved
   into work. The bot face's fate rides this too.
4. **What ends a huddle** — owner act, deliverable delivered, or staleness.
   The end card is where the deliverable verdict lives.
5. **The SOUL's composition** — a written `SOUL.md` *replaces* the compiled
   harness brief today, so either the shipped default carries the four
   virtues as a complete prompt or the SOUL's semantics change to ride beside
   it; and the overlay is appended last, so a closing constraint is needed
   if the virtues must outrank the voice.

**The four mockups proposed** (the owner's own method, from the plugins-ux
rethink): **(1)** the huddle rail — active/inactive/ended, member marks,
deliverable counts, new-huddle composer; **(2)** the open huddle — head
with goal/voice/members/state, transcript with @mentions and an in-line
deliverable, the composer with the mode chips and @ picker, the pane
previewing the deliverable; **(3)** the seat — sys1's destructive verdict
rendered as data, with the approval controls and the mode's effect; **(4)** a
breakout — the parent's child huddles shown as a tree. The response offered
to fold avatars/soul into screens 1–2 and stop at three; the owner did not
choose.
