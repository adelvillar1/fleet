## Rules for this ledger

- Log on encounter: an error, warning, defect, gap, or improvement found during any Fleet work lands here the moment it is seen, with the command or probe that produced it as evidence — before any fix, and even when the fix is immediate.
- **Improvements are captured the same way**: anything noticed that works but should or could be better — a rough edge, an inconsistency, an unverified claim, a missed opportunity — is logged with what was noticed and where. Logging is not fixing and not scope: a logged improvement does not authorize changing it mid-slice unless the owner says so; the owner triages what becomes work.
- No silent dismissals: "pre-existing," "not our repo," "unrelated to the task," and "CI is red anyway" are not closures. Deferral is explicit and owner-approved.
- Close with evidence: the fixing commit, the green run, or the owner's decision is linked in the row.
- **The gate never lies**: never pipe the gate through a status-losing pipe (`cargo test | grep` reports grep's status, not cargo's). Read the exit code — `PIPESTATUS`, or the tool's own exit_code — before committing. (Added after KI-014.)
- The ledger feeds the plan: an item that blocks a slice is named in that slice; slice 13 automates the evidence gathering, but this file stays the system of record.
