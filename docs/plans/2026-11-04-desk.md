# Plan: desk — where knowledge work happens

## Context

Fleet has two artifact cores and a leap of faith. Sessions treat working
code as the unit of work (tests, the calibration stream); design treats
the frame as one (deterministic checks, the review scores, the canvas).
Knowledge work — decks, documents, briefs — has the *pieces* but no
unit: `src/deck.rs` is a checked schema whose consultant rules refuse
(Pyramid, MECE, bolding), `src/documents.rs` routes the reading of
office files and PDFs (`Route::{Local, Pdf, Escalate, Unsupported}`,
`src/documents.rs:35`), and `src/extract.rs` carries a measured
verification spine — LOCATE → SEGMENT → VERIFY → RECONCILE, claims
asserted at p ≥ 0.85 and vetoed at p ≤ 0.35 (`src/extract.rs:68-69`),
the pattern jobhound measured. But nothing treats "produce a checked,
presentable deliverable" as a first-class loop: a deck is generated from
a prompt and trusted.

The owner handed a handoff brief (2026-11-04) whose code claims were
verified against the tree the same day, and ruled the name: **desk** —
"kind of where knowledge work happens in the real world". The desk names
the *place* (the loop: brief → claims → outline → render → review →
knowledge); `deliverable` stays the artifact term, the way huddles keeps
"room" and design keeps "frame".

The owner's same-day deep dive on the lanes and jobhound's stacked
calls (`docs/architecture/lanes-deep-dive-2026-11-04.md`) sizes the
lane work: what each service exposes, what it costs, and the measured
disciplines the criteria below carry.

## Direction lock

**The desk is a place, not an inventory.** A real desk has sources on
one side, the draft in the middle, the marked-up review on top. The
surface reads in that order: the shelf of desk projects (what was
produced, for which brief, its review state, its evidence status), the
brief composer, and the deliverable with its stage rail — each stage's
answer visible, every refusal named. Fleet's paper-and-ink skin carries
it: page/surface/ink/accent aliases, New York serif for the desk's name,
no new palette, no new tokens — the same ruling as the huddles room.

**What it is NOT.** Not a file manager. Not a rich-text editor — the
deliverable's text changes go through the run, the way a frame's page
changes go through `frame`. Not Slack's skin and not the design canvas's
boards. And one word is kept unmistakable: **desk** is the surface, a
**deck** is one artifact a desk project produces — the one-letter
adjacency gets a named rule in the code (`desk_*` keys, `deck` unchanged)
and a harness pin.

**The project is the universe.** Sessions, design, huddles, and desk
are where work happens and how the owner gains access to it — not
containers of truth. An artifact originates in one surface and is
invoked in others: the owner's own example — a frontend design
originates in design, is invoked in a huddle, and ends up in a
presentation done in the desk. The desk therefore treats the other
surfaces' artifacts as first-class references, carried with their
origin and rendered live by the machinery that owns them — and the
desk's own deliverables are referenceable the same way (C20).

**The composer.** The brief composer lives on the desk page directly —
one composer, three fields (the audience, the claim, the sources), the
design canvas's "one composer" idiom. It applies on submit: the
immediacy ruling (a control that applies only to a later run is an
error).

## Precedents this plan answers

- **Mockups first** (plugins-ux, huddles-ux): the screens are drawn,
  measured *and read as PNGs* (the 2026-11-04 lesson: the read catches
  what the measurement calls green and the measurement catches what the
  eye cannot), and the owner reads them before face code.
- **The sessions split is the one split idiom** (`docs/features/gui.md`):
  the desk page reuses the work-column machinery rather than inventing a
  second.
- **The project-knowledge ruling** (huddles C15–C17, the owner's "a
  huddle is a knowledge space"): a desk project ends by writing what it
  learned — a digest through the same memory writer, the corpus refresh
  riding it. No new store: memory and the project corpus are the
  substrates.
- **K6, designs-are-project-knowledge** (`src/canvas.rs:505-509`): a
  board bound to a project folder puts a bounded digest where a run
  born in that project reads it in assembled context. The desk extends
  the same ruling to knowledge work — the owner's 2026-11-04 ruling
  names the destination: deliverables become integral parts of the
  project's knowledge, indexed and embedded, referenced by skills, and
  composed with huddles in both directions.
- **Hard rule 12, self-containment**: extraction stays network-free and
  local (`documents.rs`'s contract unchanged); URL sources are captured
  by the run's own `web` tool (`src/tools.rs:113`) into the desk
  project's source set, so a claim still carries a local sentence.
  Nothing foreign ships, builds, or is fetched outside the app.
- **The three render targets carry their existing contracts unchanged**:
  deck's Pyramid/MECE/bolding law, the canvas's project-binding law, the
  documents' local/escalate routing. The desk adds the loop over them;
  it rewrites none of them.
- **Hard rule 11, UX co-equal**: the huddles surface shipped scaffolding
  once and the owner called it horrible; the mockup bar and the harness
  pins are this plan's defense.
- **The icons source pin** (icons C3): the harness fails on any key
  `ICONS` does not draw — the desk's sixteenth rail mark is enforced the
  moment the route appears, not by goodwill.

## Approach

One path: the desk is a new module over machinery that exists, with the
surface reusing the split idiom and the spine reusing the extraction
leg. The lane work is shaped by
`docs/architecture/lanes-deep-dive-2026-11-04.md` — what each service
exposes, and the stacked-call disciplines jobhound measured.

1. **The store first** (`src/desk.rs`): the desk project document at
   `<home>/desk/<slug>/project.json` — brief, sources with provenance,
   stages, claim set, review verdicts, digest pointer, and the project
   binding — under the filelock the other stores use, with the
   `fleet desk` card reading it.
2. **The capture seam**: a URL source is fetched by the run's `web` tool
   into `<desk>/<slug>/sources/` and recorded (url, fetched-at, title) —
   the extraction spine stays network-free, and captured text rides the
   reference-data-only framing wherever it enters a prompt.
3. **The claim set**: each source walks the extraction spine
   (`src/extract.rs`); the verify pass gains the claim-kind labels and
   the batched seam for long sources (C6); the store holds every claim
   with its source path, sentence, kind, confidence, and floor verdict.
4. **The coverage beat**: one sys1 judgment over the brief and the claim
   digest — a vendored task, an anchored score head, the judge beat's
   spend cap; named gaps ride the outline.
5. **The document law and the number leg**: the unsourced-assertion
   check in `desk.rs` refuses by name before render; decks run
   `deck.rs`'s checks, which are not modified; and a deliverable's
   figures are computed by `sdm1_classify` over real tables with the
   canonical-number guard on the drafted prose (C11).
6. **The surface** (`src/gui/`, `app.js`/`app.css`): the `#/desk` route,
   the GROUPS row, the `desk` icon key, the shelf with its states, the
   three-field composer that applies on submit, the stage rail, the
   review view, the render targets (pane deck, markdown, frame).
7. **The knowledge, the binding, and the shapes**: the distil action
   writes the digest fact through the memory writer and the corpus
   refresh embeds it; the bounded `desk_context_section` rides the
   assembled context of runs born in the bound project (K6's pattern);
   the surfaces' seam lands (references into the desk — frame, session,
   huddle — the desk card in a room, the hand-to-the-desk action);
   `src/skills.rs` gains the optional `shapes:` read so the presenter
   seed's shape range narrows skill discovery, and the tool router's
   sem1 seam ranks the library when no shape routes.
8. **Prove and deliver**: the harness cases, the four-command gate by
   exit code, the live proof on a scratch home, then the delivered
   `~/.fleet/bin/fleet` (copied through a new inode, the agent
   restarted, `#/desk` served), the rebuilt Tauri dmg, and the contract
   docs in the same delivery.

The order is the plan's own and the plan-surface's soft linearization
agreed; the mockups (C1) sit ahead of all face work and the store ahead
of everything that touches it.

## The design

**The project universe.** The binding is what makes the universe real:
everything the four surfaces do keys to the same project — the bound
folder's final segment — so a run born in the project carries the
design account (K6), the desk account (C13), and recalls the project's
sessions through the archive's own ranker (`src/session_recall.rs`)
beside its facts. A desk deliverable names its origins: claims carry
their sources, and the composition carries **references** to the
artifacts it presents — the design frame, the session, the huddle —
each with its origin surface recorded and rendered live by the owning
surface's machinery. A reference is not a copy: no pixels move into
`deck.rs`, whose own head marks an image engine as "later"
(`src/deck.rs:8`); the deliverable's reference card renders beside the
pane's live artifact.

**The store.** One desk project is one JSON document at
`<home>/desk/<slug>/project.json` — canvas's law
(`src/canvas.rs:275`), restated: brief (audience, claim, sources with
provenance), stages, the claim set, review verdicts, the digest
pointer. **Every desk project binds to a workspace project and its
folder** — the board's binding law (`src/canvas.rs:211-221`: the
project folder's absolute path, the key its final segment); the shelf
groups by project, and unbound desk work is allowed but second-class.
Artifacts are siblings (the rendered deck's HTML, the document's
markdown), and an explicit delivery can write one into the bound
project folder itself — where the corpus refresh's own docs globs find
it. States (active / parked / ended) derive from the log, never from
memory.

**The sources.** The composer accepts three kinds. **Files** read
through `documents.rs`'s routing, unchanged. **Facts** ride memory
recall — the fact path is the source. **URLs** are captured by the run's
`web` tool into `<desk>/<slug>/sources/` with provenance recorded (url,
fetched-at, title) — the capture is a run action under the approval
gate, and the extraction spine never does network itself. The boundary
is named in code: `extract.rs` reads local text, period.

**The evidence spine.** Each source walks the extraction pipeline as it
stands (`src/extract.rs`): cheap deterministic passes first, one
decision call only where evidence lives, the asymmetric reconcile
floors. A claim is (text, source path, the sentence it was read from,
confidence, verdict). Asserted, vetoed, or contested-and-marked — never
fabricated. The review view renders the contested span *beside the claim
it came from*.

**The coverage beat.** One sys1 judged call grades the claim set
against the brief's claim: what the sources do not answer is a **named
gap** in the outline, never a filled hole. (The decision-model check:
VERIFY and RECONCILE already ride sys1 inside `extract.rs`; the coverage
beat is the one new judgment, and the escalation lane is the contested
set reaching the owner's eyes in review. The value it pays is accuracy —
the gap is the alternative to a hallucinated section.)

**The document law.** `src/desk.rs` adds the refusing check prose never
had: **an unsourced assertion refuses to render as a statement** — a
paragraph asserting something no source sentence carries is cut to an
open question or marked, and the refusal names the near-miss sentence it
found. The paraphrase limitation is stated at the definition, the way
the fold's survival check states its own. Decks keep `deck.rs`'s law
byte-identical; the outline stage runs those checks *before* render and
a refusal stops the stage by name.

**The render targets.** Deck → the `deck.rs` schema, rendered by the
pane's existing deck renderer — a produced deck is visible in-product,
the desk's answer to the PPTX "honest partial" (`gui.md:118`, which
stays true for *reading foreign files*). Document → markdown through the
pane's existing renderer. Visual-heavy → the `frame` tool onto the
canvas — composition, not dependency; neither plan blocks the other.

**The knowledge digest.** At end, the distil action (the huddle's
pattern) turns the desk project's lessons into lifecycle facts through
the memory writer; the project's corpus refresh embeds the digest. The
next brief on the same project recalls them.

**The shapes vocabulary.** Half of it is already load-bearing: memory
facts carry structure and failure shapes from a compiled-in keyword
taxonomy — seven structure shapes (`deploy, build, deps, docs,
rendering, state-sync, change`) and six failure shapes
(`fail-open, drops-the-row, bound-exceeded, claim-outran-evidence,
fixed-width-clip, foreign-root` — the desk's own law is already the
fourth failure shape's name), first-keyword-wins
(`src/memory.rs:1709, 1904`); roles narrow recall by their shape range
(`src/roles.rs:44`); sys1's `curate` task routes an instruction under a
shape. Skills are the missing half: `SKILL.md` frontmatter today is
name and description only. Skills gain the optional `shapes:` line so a
persona's shape range narrows skill discovery — and the vocabulary
stays **one table**: the desk's knowledge-work kinds (presentation,
document, analysis) join the structure shapes in
`SHAPE_KEYWORDS`, so a skill, a role, and a fact all route under the
same names and the measured homonym failure (a same-named shape from
the wrong axis outranking the right answer, `src/memory.rs:47`) cannot
repeat across a second vocabulary. The desk project records the shape
its brief routes under — the importer's own keyword rule — and that
shape is what selects the declared methodology skills. **And the
undefined-shape case has its own layer**: when no shape keyword routes
— and for skill discovery generally — the library ranks through the
tool router's proven seam (`src/tool_router.rs`): the request plus each
skill's name-and-description embed, sem1 ranks the candidates by
meaning, and the lexical ranker is the named fallback when the
embeddings lane is unreachable. Shapes select the family; vectors rank
the long tail; the 60-skill cap bounds the declaration. Skills are
today the one corpus without an embedder index — memory's vector
channel and the session archive's embedder-keyed index are the
precedents (`src/memory.rs:98`, `src/session_recall.rs`). The seed
persona
is `presenter` (`src/roles.rs:699`); its methodology — the BKV pair,
the deliverables family — is declared as shapes, not copied into Fleet.
The library stays cross-harness-pure at `~/.agents/skills`; proposals
still land in `<FLEET_HOME>/skills/proposals/` and apply through the
gate, never silently (`src/skills.rs:67`).

- [ ] **C4 — the captured source.** A URL source is fetched by the run's
      `web` tool into `<desk>/<slug>/sources/` with url/fetched-at/title

- [ ] **C5 — the claim set.** Every claim in the store carries its
      source path, the sentence it was read from, a confidence, and a
      verdict from the 0.85/0.35 floors; a contested claim ships marked,

      extraction spine's output.
- [ ] **C6 — the claim kinds and the batched verify.** The verify pass
      labels each segment's kind — claim / support / background /
      boilerplate — so background prose never becomes a phantom claim
      (jobhound's measured lesson), and a source whose carrying segments
      exceed the per-call bound rides a **batched verifier** — one call
      per chunk of segments, chunks sized to the measured request caps —
      with its own named assertion floor (a batched probability's floor
      is lower than the per-segment margin's: 0.70 vs 0.85, the jobhound
      split). Floors are per-verifier named constants; nothing reuses
      one floor across providers. Pinned by a Rust test.
- [ ] **C7 — the coverage beat.** One sys1 judgment grades the claim set
      against the brief: a vendored task (the `tool_route`/`curate`
      seam) whose state carries the brief and the claim-set digest, an
      anchored score head plus gap nouls, at the family's named starting
      floor, bounded by the judge beat's spend-cap pattern
      (`src/judge.rs:91-113`), riding the calibration stream so the
      day's desk judgments are gradeable. The outline renders the named
      gaps as gaps; a run whose sources answer nothing produces the
      honest empty answer, not a fabricated outline. Pinned by a test on
      the mock and shown in the live proof.
- [ ] **C8 — the document law.** The unsourced-assertion check refuses
      by name, naming the near-miss sentence; decks run `deck.rs`'s
      checks before render and a refusal stops the stage by name;
      `deck.rs` itself is unchanged (the diff touches nothing in it).
- [ ] **C9 — the outline stage.** The outline renders for the owner
      before render target selection, and the deliverable's stage rail
      shows every stage's state (claims / outline / render / review /
      knowledge) from the log.
- [ ] **C10 — the render targets.** A produced deck renders in the pane
      (in-product, before export); a document renders as markdown; a
      visual deliverable lands a frame via the `frame` tool and the
      canvas repaints. Each target's existing contract unchanged.
- [ ] **C11 — the number leg.** A deliverable's figures are computed,
      never generated: a figure that answers "how much / how likely /
      what trend" comes from `sdm1_classify` over a real table (an
      inline background stage — the fit queue is seconds-scale, never
      inline gating), and every metric-shaped number in the drafted
      prose must appear canonically in the givens (the composed lane's

      Pinned by a Rust test.
- [ ] **C12 — the review view.** Claims render with their source

## Out of scope

- Client-side rendering of *foreign* PPTX/DOCX — the pane's office
  reading story stays the honest partial; the desk closes only produced-
  deliverable visibility.
- In-place rich-text editing, collaborative cursors, version trees —
  the deliverable's text changes go through the run.
- A desk-owned vector store or a new persistence mechanism — the corpus
  refresh and memory are the substrates.
- Threads, reactions, or any huddles machinery — sibling surfaces
  compose; neither plan blocks the other.
- Narration or transcription on the desk's surface — gen1's `speak` and
  `transcribe` legs exist in the library but are not on the MCP
  surface; that is a re-vendor/_upstream question, not this plan's.
- Data-bound charts from `gen1_imagine` — the lane is prompt-to-image
  only; the desk's figures render from computed numbers through the
  deck's own components.
- Modifying `deck.rs`, `documents.rs`, `canvas.rs`, or
  `~/.agents/skills` files.
- Embedding other surfaces' pixels inside the deck schema (deck.rs's
  image engine is its own marked "later" and deck.rs stays unchanged) —
  a reference card beside the pane's live artifact is the desk's answer.
- A full cross-surface artifact registry (every surface declaring every
  artifact's origin and references in one store) — the universe's
  read seams (K6 digests, session recall, the desk store) are the v1;
  a registry is its own plan if the owner wants one.

## Risks

- **The extraction spine's cost on long sources.** Per-segment verify
  is bounded by the carrying-segment cap, and C6's batched seam is the
  answer for sources beyond it — but chunking has a measured failure
