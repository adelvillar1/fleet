work · the face

Every icon, one meaning.

Each card is a key in the face's table. The four sizes are the sizes it is drawn at — 13 in a transcript card, 14 in the palette, 17 in a rail, 18 in a section rail. A redrawn key shows was above and its replacement below. Thirteen keys today have no drawing at all and fall through to the same hollow ring; they appear here for the first time.
