The rail is a map, not a menu

Three regions, named for the kind of work they exist for — making software, making design, making knowledge. The geography is stable, so the picture is learnable; the region tells you what kind of work a lane serves before the lane’s own name does.

Attention is not a region any more. The Home row at the head carries what is held; the lanes keep only a dot, and the board keeps its needs-you lead. The inbox and the board are the attention surfaces — the rail is the place you navigate from.

  • Desk and Reviews are both evidence work — a claim read against its source, a judgment graded against its evidence.
  • Design stands alone: the canvas is the only design instrument.
  • Fleet rides software: the delivery instances.
  • System is a foot strip — never a work region.
