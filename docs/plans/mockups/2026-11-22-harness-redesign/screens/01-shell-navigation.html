The drawer, the contextual reading surface one drawer per stage · opens by press · follows the focus (U3)

What the pane did in four mixed duties, the drawer does as one grammar with tabs:

  • read — a card's, row's, or node's whole document (the pane-body duty)
  • act — the actions that belong to the focus (approve, supersede, restore, revoke)
  • context — what the focus connects to: the connections tab (U11's tissue, named for what it does — U42)
  • made here — what the focused conversation generated (U39's receipts, renamed per U42)

Below 1180px the drawer is the sheet sliding over the stage; the rail stays. Both keep a 30px edge tab as the way back (U12's law: collapsed is a strip, not a vanish).
