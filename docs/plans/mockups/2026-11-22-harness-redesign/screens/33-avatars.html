The borrowing, named self-containment, hard rule 12
The component is vendored in-repo (single file, zero dependencies, ~56 KB) behind a persona-face seam; the upstream is MIT and its attribution lives in the vendor notice.
Flat tint mode is the default (D8) — personas take their color from the token chain at runtime. The 188 skins carry fixed colors that would fight the theme contract; they stay off until you ask per persona, and enabling one records an explicit exemption from the no-hex guard.
Product copy never names the upstream character (U42) — it is the persona's face, tinted, stateful, and yours.
Motion (A3): reduced-motion is honored until your first motion press, then the stored choice wins; pointer-follow is desktop-only and off under reduce; auto-sleep after the idle window.
