# The Learning mockup — measurements

The surface: the rail's Learning section (the lesson with the badge, the
graph, practice, the history) and the lesson pane (the coarse plan with
the current position, the held propositions).

Rendered at 900x600 in the app's own tokens (app.css), the four themes.

| theme | contrast (min) | overflow | ink | colors |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| paper | 14.79 | 0 | 1.0% | 91 |
| console | 13.44 | 0 | 99.0% | 92 |
| graphite | 15.09 | 0 | 99.0% | 82 |
| contrast | 18.10 | 0 | 1.1% | 69 |

The contrast is the minimum text/background ratio across the rail rows,
the nodes, the propositions, and the position line (the in-page
computation); the overflow counts the elements whose scrollWidth passes
their clientWidth; the ink ratio and the color count come from the PNG
pixels (the light pair's grounds, the dark pair's grounds — the themes'
palettes are distinct).

The PNGs: paper/console/graphite/contrast at 900x600. The read of the
PNGs: the image input was unavailable in the building session, so the
read is the programmatic one above; the owner's read of the surface is
the criterion's last leg.
