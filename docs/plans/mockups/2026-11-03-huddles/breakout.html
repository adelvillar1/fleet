What the child carries

a breakout is a session under the parent, not a copy
the parent’s goal every child turn serves the whole brief, not just its phase
the phase brief written at the break — what this room is for, in your words or the bot’s
the guardrail your SOUL rides every child’s turns
its own members inherited from the parent, and the room can convene more
its own deliverables posted in the child’s room, rolled up into the parent’s count
its own ending ending a child does not end the parent

A breakout exists when the work is multi-phase or splits into another deliverable. It is the delegation seam made visible: the child is a session under the parent’s, so the log already knows who spawned it and the graph can draw the tree.
