How trust works

Everything on this page is a plugin. The tiers differ in what the bundle may reach — and who is answerable for it.

privileged

You vouched for it. A bundle you wrote or reviewed — it mounts as a first-class surface with every route the face can, writes included. Installing one is an act that names the grant; the registry records it.

untrusted

Generated, sandboxed. Creator mode's output — its card is a frame on the network-dead route, its data only the read-only queries its manifest declares, enforced by the bridge. It never writes, and it never elevates itself.

draft the static suite must pass installed · untrusted the whole suite + your elevation act privileged

The suite grades; your act moves. A failing evaluation refuses the promotion by name, and the registry records every step with its verdict.
