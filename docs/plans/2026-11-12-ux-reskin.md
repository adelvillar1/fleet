# Plan: the U-reskin — the production faces under the U-ledger

## Context

The UX trilogy is approved: three working reference mockups
(`docs/design/fleet-onboarding-mockup.html`, `fleet-workspace-mockup.html`,
`armada-workspace-mockup.html`) and the design-system ledger
[`UI-DECISIONS.md`](UI-DECISIONS.md) at **U0–U41** — the owner's words: *"the
design is approved"*, with the amendments recorded (pinned composer U20, the
thread pane U39, Intelligence U40, the system-one step U41). The production
faces still render the old system. This plan implements the ledger across the
real surfaces: the browser face (`src/gui/assets/`), the TUI, the CLI verbs and
`fleet onboard` — with fleet-desktop inheriting the face automatically (U29:
no second frontend, no second transport). Every phase cites the `U` ids it
implements and ships at the visual bar (hard rule 11), verified against the
mockups side by side.

## Design choices inherited (the ledger)

Conforms to **U0–U41** — in particular U4 (the eight-signal palette law, no
tenth hue), U5 (tokens as custom properties, both themes, zero hardcoded
literals), U9 (the shell grid), U29 (armada additive), U30–U33 (desk loop,
calibration word, memory dream, learning), U34–U38 (setup as graph growth),
U39 (the thread pane), U40 (Intelligence), U41 (at minimum one system-one
model). And **D0–D6**, hard rule 5 (reconstructable from the log), hard rule 11.

## Decision-model check

No sys1 head fits: theming, layout, and rendering are deterministic plumbing;
quality is graded by the face harness, the four-leg gate, and the visual check
against the mockups — never by a per-item judgment.

## The owner's steer (2026-11-12, mid-flight): the overhaul, not a reskin

The owner reviewed the landed cuts and ruled: *"I'm worried that the project
you are executing is just a reskinning and not the complete overhaul of the
UX that was the project's original intent."* The audit agrees: the visual
system and several panes are real, but the approved design's CORE — U3's one
focus as the shell's law, the lenses as views over the graph, the dispatch
hero flow, U12's console, U24's two doors — is not wired, and the surfaces
still read as separate boards. The remaining work re-centers on the
structural spine, in this order:

1. **The focus law** — one `focusOn(kind, id)`; the tissue, the focus card
   and every lens follow one pivot. The foundation.
2. **The dispatch hero flow** — dispatch streams in the workspace; the
   graph grows the edges in the same beat.
3. **The console region + chrome search** (U12/U24).
4. Then the TUI/desktop color law and onboarding completion (Cuts 5/6).

## The owner's steer (2026-11-12, second): the shell is not workspace-centric

The owner reviewed the delivered v0.7.3 against the mockups and ruled: *"but
this is not workspace centric, the mockups are."* The audit agrees, and this
one is **architectural**: `pivotTo` still navigates the hash to the object's
home panel (`window.location.hash = '#/' + target.lens`) and every panel
fetches its own store (`/api/tasks`, `/api/desk`, `/api/memory`…). The
S-series delivered *focus joins the silos* — a connections sidebar and a focus
law bolted onto the 22-panel surface-swap. The mockups are the other
architecture: **one workspace graph, the center always a lens over it, the
focused object constant while the view changes, the graph IS the navigation**
— which is what U0 has said all along. The sidebar honored U0's visibility;
the shell did not honor U0's structure.

**S6 — the workspace shell** closes this, in three steps:

1. **The spine is one read.** A workspace aggregate (over the existing
   stores — the L7 graph, desk, session threads, armada instances, the
   intelligence summaries) fetched once as the shell's context; every lens is
   a projection of it. No panel owns its own object store.
2. **The center is a lens renderer.** Rail entries render views over the same
   objects: board = the tasks view, desk = the deliverables view, threads =
   the focused task's thread, intelligence = the capability segments, fleet =
   the instances view. Switching lens re-projects; it never leaves the
   workspace and never blanks into a foreign panel.
3. **The conversation is the thread lens.** S2 already opens a thread for a
   dispatch — generalize: the composer belongs to the focused thread; the
   chat stops being "the" center and becomes the lens the focused work
   streams in. Machinery that is not a graph object (settings, vault,
   plugins, design, about) stays a rail surface beside the workspace.

Non-graph surfaces stay reachable; nothing the owner does is refused; the
connections column and the focus law carry over unchanged.

## Approach — the overhaul, in two layers

**The owner's comparison ruling (2026-11-12):** the original intent was a
COMPLETE UX overhaul — one workspace, one graph, every surface a lens, one
focus the whole workspace follows, dispatch streaming in the workspace — not
a reskin. The first six cuts (below, "the foundation") delivered the visual
system and several additive panes faithfully, but the plan as first written
under-scoped the intent: its "renders what exists / no app.js rewrite" framing
never made the structural core a cut. The audit (intent → plan → execution)
names the gap: U3's focus law, U0's lenses-as-graph-views, U20's dispatch flow,
U12's console, U14's board, U21's optimistic-settled, U24's chrome search,
U17's inbox, U23's bulk, U38's armada setup were never cuts.

**The structural spine is now first-class** (the overhaul proper), in
dependency order, each its own gated release:

- **S1 — the focus law (U3/U0).** One `focusOn(kind, id)`; the connections
  column, the focus card, and every lens follow one pivot. The lenses become
  views over the graph: opening a deliverable / thread / lesson IS focusing
  that node. The foundation everything composes with.
- **S2 — the dispatch hero flow (U20/U34/U38).** Dispatch streams in the
  workspace; the graph grows the edges in the same beat. Composes over
  `workspace::create_task` / `link` (existing functions; exposing them is
  plumbing, like the disposition verb).
- **S3 — the console + chrome search (U12/U24) + the armada switch (U29).**
- **S4 — the board + bulk + optimistic-settled + inbox (U14/U23/U21/U17).**
- **S5 — TUI/desktop color law (U15/U16) + armada setup (U38).**

The "no new capability / no app.js rewrite" constraint is LIFTED where it
blocks the structural work; the incremental, harness-green discipline stays.
The foundation cuts (1–6) remain the record of what is already laid.

### The foundation — six cuts, each atomic (laid)

One design token set first (nothing else can theme without it), then one
surface group per cut, the harness green at every step. Each cut is its own
release (the house rule: a user-facing change bumps the third segment).

1. **Cut 1 — tokens + shell (0.7.1, U4–U11).** `app.css` gains the token set:
   the eight signals + translucent washes, ink/panel/raise surfaces, line/line2
   translucent borders, three radii, one shadow, the three type voices, and
   the motion contract (one ambient layer, prefers-reduced-motion honored,
   a Motion switch). Theme: night default, Day Watch via `[data-theme]`, a
   chrome toggle. The shell: rail reorganized to Mission / Surfaces (live
   counts) / Saved views / tissue explainer; the lens-head pattern (title +
   sub + tools). Existing sections keep working — tokens are additive and
   components migrate section by section.
2. **Cut 2 — Intelligence (0.7.2, U40).** The Memory surface becomes
   **Intelligence** with four segments: Memories (basis ladder, anti-vectors,
   Dream — `/api/memory` + consolidation as today), **Skills** (`/api/skills`
   exists; render active/proposal states, the gate-apply action),
   **Tools** (new read: the bounded surface with builtin/lane/MCP kinds and
   read/write/destructive classes), **Workflows** (new read: chains as a stage
   track, runs graded). The tissue graph carries the intelligence family in
   teal (hue = family, icon+label = kind).
3. **Cut 3 — Threads (0.7.3, U20/U39).** The composer pinned to the bottom of
   the chat area; the transcript auto-follows only while already at the end;
   the collapsible **thread pane** listing the objects the conversation
   generated (deliverables, facts written, grades, lessons), color-dotted,
   fresh rows pulsing, click = pivot.
4. **Cut 4 — Desk + Calibration (0.7.4, U30/U31).** Deliverable cards render
   the five-stage pipeline with the current stage lit, coverage, and named
   refusals **on the card**; advance/settle wired to the existing desk
   endpoints. The calibration surface: confidence meters, graded outcomes, and
   the owner's agree-or-disagree riding the same append-only row.
5. **Cut 5 — Onboarding + setup (0.7.5, U34–U38/U41).** The setup lens on a
   fresh home — setup as graph growth, wired to the real `/api/onboard` flow:
   the install node, the account, **the judgment model step (U41: at minimum
   one system-one model; Fastino/Liquid/Drex linked to their own pages —
   Liquid is d1, never jev)**, the proof, the folder grant, and armada
   provisioning as the same gesture. Empty states name the step that fills
   them; skipped steps stay named.
6. **Cut 6 — TUI + fleet-desktop (0.7.6, U1/U15/U16/U29).** The TUI boards
   adopt the status color law and the palette pairs (the armada board is
   already visual — this is the color contract, not a rebuild); fleet-desktop
   verified wrapping the reskinned face over the same server.

## Risks

- **app.js is large and conventional** — the reskin is incremental per
  section, harness green between sections; never a big-bang rewrite.
- **Theme regression on untouched sections** — tokens are additive; a section
  only joins the system when it is migrated in its own phase.
- **The harness rail-count pin** — GROUPS changes move the destination count;
  the pin updates in the same change (KI-447's lesson: the note rides the
  state, not the DOM).
