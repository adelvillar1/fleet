# Plan: the bots page on the same rail — personas on the left, what needs you on the right

## Context

The owner's direction (2026-10-29): *"we can probably use the same pattern
on the bots page, use the rail to group sessions by persona with pending
action from the users or messages related to an end of turn."*

The bots surface today is **channel plumbing**: a service card (start,
stop, heartbeat), a pairing card, the Telegram token, the peers and the
pending peers, plus an activity line. It answers "is the transport up and
who is paired" — it does not answer the question a person actually
arrives with: **"who is working for me, and what am I being asked?"**

What the data says (this machine, 2026-10-30):

- The **channels document carries no conversation**: `channels[]` holds
  `name`, `peers`, `pending`; the rest is `service`, `telegram`,
  `activity`, `waiting_seats`. There is no session, persona, or message
  in it.
- The **sessions document already has the axis**: every row carries
  `personas` (the lead first) and, for a channel conversation,
  `channel`. Today this home holds 5 persona-bearing sessions (`tower` ×5,
  `auditor` ×1) and 0 channel sessions — the shape exists, the population
  does not yet.
- The **states the owner asked about already exist**: `needs-approval`
  (a seat is waiting — "pending action from the user") and `handed back`
  (the run ended its turn and awaits the owner — "a message related to an
  end of turn"). Both are already derived by `work_state` and counted by
  the archive.
- The **personas are a registry**, not a list to copy: `roles/` in the
  repo and `~/.fleet/roles/` (six: `tower`, `clerk`, `auditor`, `scholar`,
  `presenter`, `advocate`), read by `fleet roles` and by the delegation
  path.

So this is not a new design — it is the **bot-PWA ruling applied to the
console surface**: *assistant-centric, personas are the axis* (2026-10-28),
with the settings revamp's rail idiom (`2026-10-29-settings-rail.md`)
reused rather than reinvented.

## Decisions (the owner's, dated and quoted)

1. **2026-10-29 — the rail.** *"we can probably use the same pattern on
   the bots page, use the rail to group sessions by persona with pending
   action from the users or messages related to an end of turn."*
2. **2026-10-29 — the pane's depth.** *"the right side pane could carry
   the entire conversation details, history and action items or
   decisions."*

## Approach

### The shape

The page becomes **rail of personas → that persona's conversations →
the conversation itself**:

```
channels │ tower    (2 need you)   │  needs you
         │ scholar  (1 turned back)│  ─ the session, the state, the ask
         │ clerk    (—)            │  ended its turn, awaiting you
         │ auditor  (—)            │  ─ the last message, clipped
         │ presenter(—)            │  earlier
         │ advocate (—)            │
         │ unassigned (3)          │
```

### The rail's rows

1. **One row per persona**, derived from the roles registry — name, the
   persona's own mark (the inline serif-initial on a lane tint the bot
   design established), and a badge counting **what needs the owner in
   that persona** (`needs-approval` + `handed back`). No badge when
   nothing does; an empty badge is decoration.
2. **`unassigned`** — sessions with no persona (console work that landed
   here), named rather than hidden.
3. **`channels`** — the plumbing the page shows today, unchanged in
   substance: service, pairing, Telegram token, peers, pending peers,
   activity. It is a section, not the whole page.

The rail is the settings revamp's rail: collapse to icons, remembered,
`aria-current`, arrow keys. **One implementation, two surfaces** — see
*The shared rail* below.

### A persona's section

Sessions as one-line rows, ordered by what the owner must do, then by
recency:

- **`needs you`** — state `needs-approval`: the row names the ask (the
  tool and its summary), carries the **approve / deny** actions inline
  (the destructive gate is the only thing that holds, per the 2026-10-28
  ruling), and opens the conversation in the pane.
- **`turned back`** — state `handed back`: the run ended its turn and is
  waiting. The row shows the **last message clipped** — that is the
  "message related to an end of turn" — with its age, and a press opens
  the conversation to continue it.
- **`recent`** — everything else in that persona, one line each, folded
  behind a count when long (the settings revamp's row budget, same
  helper).

A row is one line: mark, title, state word, age, and (in `needs you`) its
two actions.

### The conversation opens in the pane — details, history, decisions

The settings revamp's item card, used again: a session's press opens it
**in the right pane**, the tabs standing down while it is open. The card
carries the whole conversation in three parts, and **every part is
already in a read the face makes** — the replay (`/api/session?id=`) and
the state document (`/api/state?session=`) — so nothing is invented to
fill it:

1. **The details** — persona(s) and the join order, the model that
   served, `created`/`ended`, the exchanges and turns, the workspace,
   and the state word. All from the replay's own head plus the summary
   row the list already carried.
2. **The decisions and asks** — the run's own record, filterable from the
   same cards:
   - the **pending approval** when the state carries a seat (the ask, and
     approve / deny in the destructive class),
   - the **standing goal** (the `goal` cards) — what this conversation was
     for,
   - the **facts it decided to remember** (the `memory` cards) — each one
     a decision with its name and line, and a press to open it in the
     memory panel,
   - the **last message** when `stop_reason` is a handback — the ask the
     run ended on.
   Each is one line with its evidence; none is a summary a model wrote.
3. **The history** — the entire transcript, newest first, in pages of
   fifty cards with "load earlier", tool rows **collapsed to one line
   each** (the transcript's own idiom), so a 200-card session reads as a
   scannable column instead of a wall. The `omitted` count the replay
   carries is named where the read had to cut.

The work surface keeps the live conversation; the pane is the
read-decide-act surface for a conversation being triaged from this page,
and `#/bots/<persona>/<session>` addresses it. Below 1180 px the pane is
the overlay object cards already use.

### The data: the channels read grows a conversations half

The page needs the sessions the channels document does not carry, and it
must not read 200 rows to show 20. The bridge's `/api/channels` grows:

```jsonc
{
  "personas": [
    { "name": "tower", "sessions": [
        { "id": "…", "title": "…", "state": "needs-approval",
          "age": "12m", "last": "the clipped last line", "channel": "telegram" }
      ], "needs": 1, "turned_back": 1 }
  ],
  "unassigned": [ … ],
  "channels": [ … ]            // unchanged
}
```

- **Derived, never restated**: the persona list comes from
  `roles::load_all`; the sessions come from the same `session::list`
  summaries the archive uses (`personas`, `channel`, `state` via
  `work_state`).
- **The last line rides the bounded tail read** the inbox already
  uses (16 KiB, newest sayable line) — no new reader, no full replay.
- **Bounded**: the newest N (50) persona-bearing or channel sessions,
  the bound carried on the document so a miss is honest.
- Channels with no persona join `unassigned` rather than a phantom row.

### The shared rail

The settings revamp's R0 builds the rail; this plan **consumes it**. To
keep one implementation rather than two copies, R0 extracts the piece
both need as a small helper in `app.js`:

- `sectionRail(declaration, options)` — rows of (icon, name, badge,
  changed-mark), collapse-to-icons with a persistence key, `aria-current`,
  arrow roving, and the row-budget helper for long lists.

The settings plan calls it with eight sections; this plan calls it with
personas + `unassigned` + `channels`. If the two land apart, the second
consumer is what proves the helper is a helper — so this plan names the
dependency instead of duplicating the rail.

## Increments

### B0 — the rail and the sections (needs the settings rail)

The page becomes rail + one section; the rail's rows are the personas
(derived), `unassigned`, and `channels`; the channels section holds the
existing cards unchanged. Sections render their sessions as plain rows
by state. `#/bots/<section>` addresses a section.

*Checks:* the rail's rows equal the registry's roles + two fixed rows;
collapse/remember/keys; the route.

### B1 — what needs you, and what ended its turn

The two orderings and their rows: `needs you` with inline approve/deny,
`turned back` with the clipped last message; the counts on the rail's
rows; a section's row budget when the persona's list is long.

*Checks:* a fixed document renders the two groups in that order, the
actions post the existing approval op, the badge counts match the groups,
and the budget's count is honest.

### B2 — the conversation in the pane

The row's press opens the session in the pane (tabs stood down), Escape
returns focus, and `#/bots/<persona>/<session>` addresses it.

*Checks:* open/close/focus/route, and that the pane shows the same tail
the work surface does.

### B3 — the bridge's conversations half

`/api/channels` grows `personas`/`unassigned` with the derived,
bounded, tail-read shape; the face switches from composing two reads to
the one document. The bound is carried and named.

*Checks:* the plan's own integration test over a scratch home with
persona-bearing and channel sessions; the bound; the derivation (a new
role appears in the rail without an edit).

## Out of scope

- **The bot-PWA itself** (the phone shell). This is the console's bots
  surface; the PWA keeps its own screens.
- **A conversation state machine.** The states are the archive's own
  (`needs-approval`, `handed back`); the page renders them.
- **`unassigned` backfill.** Sessions without a persona stay unassigned;
  assigning one is a separate act (a `join` record), not this page's job.
- **Unread counts.** The archive has no read/unread notion; "needs you"
  and "turned back" are the honest two, and inventing a third would be a
  state the log cannot support.
- **Model-extracted action items.** The card's decisions are the run's
  own records (seats, goals, remembered facts, the ending ask). Pulling
  "to-dos" out of prose with a model is the extraction leg's job
  (`docs/plans/2026-10-19-sys1-extraction-leg.md`, E3) and would put an
  unverifiable summary beside verified rows — named, not slipped in.

## Verification

The harness carries B1–B2 against a fixed channels document (the
personas, the two orderings, the actions, the badges, the budget, the
pane). The bridge's half is a scratch-home integration test with three
persona-bearing sessions and one channel session — the derivation, the
bound, and the tail line. The gate runs whole after every increment with
exit codes read directly, and the live pass measures the page as it is
today against the three sections it becomes.

## Landed 2026-10-30

One commit (`ef9b10f`) carried B0–B3, because the surface and its data
are one shape: the rail's rows cannot be planned without the document
that names the personas.

- **The rail** is the settings revamp's own `sectionRail` — its second
  consumer, which is what made it a helper. Personas come from
  `roles::load_all`, so a seventh role appears with no code change; the
  live home renders six plus `channels`.
- **The orderings**: `needs you` (`needs-approval`, with the state word
  on the row), `turned back` (`handed back`, carrying the run's last
  line), `recent` (budgeted at eight).
- **The card** in the pane reads the existing replay: the details from
  its head, the decisions from the `goal` and `memory` cards plus the
  ending ask, and the history as the whole transcript newest-first with
  tool rows on one line and the replay's `omitted` count named. On this
  machine the tower conversation's card carried 24 records.
- **The bridge's conversations half** derives everything it shows and
  binds the cost: 50 rows, the last line from the 16 KiB tail read the
  inbox already uses, the bound on the document.

The harness had **no `/api/channels` stub at all** — the bots cases were
passing against a default — so the stub, an `emptyChannels()` document
and a re-pointed pairing case (`#/bots/channels`) landed with this.
Harness 416 checks across 45 cases; gate green.

**Not done, named**: assigning an unassigned conversation is still a
separate act (the plan's out-of-scope), and model-extracted action items
stay out until the extraction leg lands.
